# TASTE — GMS Rising Petals admissions promo (style memory; read first in every chat)
Status: built at the owner's request after story approval ("approved"); look locked to the client's own brand.

## 1. Brief and concept
- New preschool GMS Rising Petals, Tumkur. Admissions promo, 9:16 1080×1920, 60 fps, 46.5 s, English VO (ElevenLabs "Anika", assets/vo/vo_v2.mp3) + music bed (assets/music/music_bed.mp3, faded 44.3–46.5 s) + SFX.
- Pipeline B: client-style 3D cartoon characters/props/backgrounds generated by the owner (assets/images), keyed and composited in code; all type, icons, transitions, petals, flowers and the logo build drawn in code.
- Idea: "every big dream begins as a tiny seed" — one petal is the through-line; everything grows, blooms or rises.

## 2. Look
- Brand colours: green #0f7a43 / deep #0b5a31, red #d9262e, orange #f7941d, yellow #ffc928, cream #fff8ec. Program colours from the flyer: pink #e93f80 (Playhome), orange #f47b20 (Nursery), blue #1f6fe0 (LKG), purple #7b2fbe (UKG).
- Type: Fredoka 600–700 (titles, "sticker" style: fill + thick outline + soft shadow), Nunito 800–900 (numbers, labels).
- Logo: the client's file, split into layers (scripts/logo_layers.py): disc, rings (draw on), GMS ribbon (wipe write-on), name + tagline (centre-out wipes).
- Safe area: content y 240–1500.

## 3. Motion
- Transitions all petal/nature family: whip-up, push-through, scalloped iris, portal through the logo, paper rise, grass wipe, cloud burst, flower iris.
- Pops with 6–9 % overshoot; stems grow from the ground; children bob, sway, peek and jump.

## 4. Sound
- Music bed ducked 7 dB under the voice; 69 SFX cues (assets/sfx/cues.json) synthesised by the Baqur reel kit synth (scripts/render_sfx.py). Climax: "Open!" (hit + sub + chime). Master −13.5 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP.

## 5. Technical
- Render: `MVD_TOOLKIT=/opt/node-tools node src/render.js --fps 60 --scale 1 --out renders/final --name "<name>" --audio renders/audio/mix.wav`
- Sound: `node <skill>/framewright/fw-sfx.mjs assets/sfx/cues.json` · keying: `python scripts/key.py` · timing: plan/beats.json.

## 6. Do / don't
- Only facts from the client's flyers (programs, ages, dates, values, address, numbers). No fees, no results.
- Drawn school scenes are illustrations; never present them as the real campus.
