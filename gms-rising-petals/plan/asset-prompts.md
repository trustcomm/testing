Generate in this order. Make **image 1 first**, then attach it as the reference image for every image after it, so the
characters and the look stay the same everywhere. Any good image model works (ChatGPT image, Gemini / Nano Banana,
Higgsfield, Midjourney). Download at the largest size offered, PNG if possible, and send them with the file names below.

Rules for every image:
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image (I add all text and the logo in the video).
- Characters and props on a flat pure chroma-green background (#00B140), evenly lit, no shadow on the background, with
  space around the subject — I cut them out. The boy and girl wear no green, so the cut-out stays clean.
- Backgrounds are vertical 9:16 and empty of people (the characters are added in the video).

Shared style line (already inside each prompt): *cute 3D cartoon illustration, soft Pixar-like rendering, rounded
shapes, soft studio light, warm cheerful colours, clean and high detail*.
