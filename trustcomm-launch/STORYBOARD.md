# trustcomm launch film: storyboard

1920×1080 · 30 fps · 47.0 s · Google-style product launch: light canvas, soft blurred colour
fields, rounded cards, springy "Material" motion and colour iris wipes. Music is 120 BPM and
every scene cut lands on a bar line (5, 11, 17, 31, 37, 41 s).

| Scene | Window | Idea | Transition out |
|-------|--------|------|----------------|
| S1 Hook | 0.0–5.0 | Four brand-colour dots bounce in, merge into a gold ★ (burst). "Your customers love your store. / But most never leave a review." (marker sweep) | Blue iris from the star |
| S2 Problem | 5.0–11.4 | "Asking for reviews is a **headache**." Friction cards pop in: search-us, scroll/sign-in, a long review URL types out, "I'll do it later → NEVER HAPPENS", a sad 3.9★ tile. Cards rattle, riser | Blue/yellow iris into the logo |
| S3 Reveal | 11.3–16.4 | "Meet": *trust* rises from a mask, *comm* springs out (impact + chime). Ring expands. "The easiest way to get Google reviews from your customers." + business-type chips | Blue slide panel |
| S4 How it works | 16.3–30.4 | Left: 3 steps (Scan / Rate / Post) with a moving highlight. Right: QR counter stand + phone. Scan (scanline, ✓ beep) → review page slides in → 5 stars fill (rising twinkles) → note types → finger tap on "Review on Google" → "Review posted!" → stand swaps to a Google-style listing; new 5★ review flies in, count 248→249 | Blue/green iris |
| S5 Results | 30.3–37.4 | "More reviews. Better rating. **More customers.**" Dashboard card: rating counts 3.9→4.8, reviews 12→249, monthly bars grow, "Top rated nearby" badge. *Illustrative example* footnote | Blue slide panel |
| S6 Kinetic | 37.3–41.4 | "No chasing." / "No awkward asks." / "Just more **5-star** reviews." (earlier lines grey out, colour burst) | Blue iris into the logo |
| S7 Endcard | 41.3–47.0 | Logo build, "Google reviews, without the headache.", CTA pill "Get started at trustcomm.app →", four bouncing dots, fade to canvas | End |

## Audio cue map (see `scripts/make_audio.py`)

- **Music:** C-major pluck/marimba arps, warm pad, soft kick, claps and shakers. Intro is sparse. S2 drops to a filtered minor pad with a riser. The full groove starts on the logo (11 s), lifts at 37 s, a clap fill leads into the endcard, and the last chord rings out.
- **SFX:** dot pops · star chime and shimmer · whooshes on every wipe · card pops · keyboard typing (URL, review note) · "uh-oh" mallets · rattle and riser · logo impacts and chimes · UI ticks · scanner hum and beep · star twinkles (rising scale) · tap click · success chime · review-fly whoosh · notification ding · counter ticks · badge sparkle · word pops · CTA pop and dot bounces.
