# Trustcomm — Instagram reel: storyboard (v1, for approval)

Format: 9:16, 1080×1920, 60 fps, ≈ 38 s. Hinglish VO (owner generates in ElevenLabs) + sound effects only (the SaaS Launch
style has no music). Audience: shop and restaurant owners in Karnataka and South India. CTA: free Google score check.
Style: MotionSmith **SaaS Launch**, *bright-flat* sub-look recoloured to Trustcomm: stage #faf9f6, ink #12151c,
muted #667085, ONE accent #1f5eff (blue). Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold headlines, Inter UI. Demo shop: Meera's Tiffin Room.
Times are estimates until the VO arrives; then every beat locks to its word timestamps.

## 1. Idea
**The blue line.** On trustcomm.app a thin blue line trails out of the "m" in the logo and connects the steps.
In the film that line is the through-line: it starts as the poll's progress bar in the hook, runs down into the QR code,
becomes the phone's scan line, draws the stars, splits into two paths (Google draft · private note), and finally sweeps
round into the score gauge in the CTA. One continuous camera ride, no slideshow cuts.

## 2. High-interaction hook (0–4 s)
An **Instagram poll sticker** fills the screen, the way people answer polls in Stories every day:
"Aaj kitne customers ne **Google review** diya?" with three options **0–2 · 3–5 · 10+**. A finger taps "0–2", the tap
ripples, the poll card wobbles. On screen, a small prompt: "Comment mein batao 👇". It asks the owner a question about
their own shop in the first second, so they answer it in their head (and in the comments). No invented numbers: it is a
question, not a statistic.

## 4. Safe and honest
- No ratings, review counts or results invented. The hook is a question; the gauge shows no number.
- Shows exactly the real flow: customer edits and posts on Google themselves; private note is offered to everyone,
  never instead of Google; no five-star promise.
- Price and offer not in this reel (CTA is the free score). Demo shop is the site's own "Meera's Tiffin Room".
- All text inside the Instagram safe zone (top 240 px, bottom 420 px, right 140 px clear).

## 5. Style notes (from MotionSmith SaaS Launch)
- Fast in, slow settle (moves ≈ 250 ms, signature moves 450–900 ms); still ratio ≈ 0.35; ≤ 2 big springs.
- One accent only (blue). Red appears once (the 1-star card) as the "alert once" colour.
- Sound: SFX only, ≈ 1.4 hits/s, whooshes lead by 130 ms, one climax (the URL), 150 ms silence before it.
- Length note: the style's sweet spot is 12–25 s; this reel is ≈ 38 s because the VO explains the flow. Each
  feature beat stays ≤ 7 s so nothing drags.
